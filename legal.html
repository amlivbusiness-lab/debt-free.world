What data we collect and why

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service you have requested. Both the free and the paid onboarding form save into the same record, so the list below covers both. Which questions you are actually asked depends on which product you are using, and every answer is optional.

Your account

  • Email address — to deliver your plan and your sign-in link
  • First name, country, language and currency — to address and localise your plan
  • Which product you have, when your account was created, when it expires, and whether you have completed onboarding
  • For paid and Community Access members: the Stripe session identifier for the purchase, and whether, and when, you accepted the terms at checkout. We never see or store your card details.

What you tell us about your money

  • Monthly income, housing costs, other monthly costs, any extra income and the amount of it, and your savings buffer
  • Your total debt, how many debts you have, and what types of debt they are
  • A list of your individual debts — up to 20 — and for each one: the type, the creditor’s name, the amount, the currency, the interest rate, the monthly payment, whether a collection agency or a court is involved, and whether the debt is secured
  • Whether you are in collections or enforcement

These are stored as the exact figures you enter, not as ranges. Ranges are used only in the anonymous statistical record described further below.

What you tell us about your situation

  • Your household type, how many children you have, your employment situation, and whether you have people around you who can help
  • Your main goal, what you want to focus on first, the biggest thing standing in your way, and how confident you feel on a scale of 0 to 10
  • If you use the letter tool: the creditor you are writing to, what you want from them, the action you are proposing, what you are most worried about, the tone you want, and any extra amount you can offer each month
  • Your companion mode and whether you want reminders

What we generate for you

  • Your personalised plan, generated by AI from the answers above
  • Any letters and documents you create, and your progress through the plan

Three questions are open text, and you can write anything in them. They are the question about your current situation (up to 500 characters), the one that invites you to describe things in your own words (400 characters), and the one at the end asking whether there is anything else we should know (500 characters). Because they are free text, you may end up writing things we never asked for — health information, or information about other people in your life. Please share only what you are comfortable sharing. What you write is sent to our AI provider to generate your plan, is stored with the rest of your answers, is deleted with them on day 90, and never enters the anonymous statistical record — free text is excluded from it by design, not by policy.

Where this is stored and why: Your answers and your plan are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV (a key-value database), reachable only through your secure access token. The purpose is to power your Member Area, show your plan on later visits, and let the tools work on your real numbers instead of asking you again. Your email address is also stored in our email system (Brevo) to deliver your plan and your sign-in links. Financial answers are transmitted to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your plan.

Note: like all internet services, our infrastructure providers (Cloudflare) may retain technical request logs for short periods for security and operational purposes. These logs do not contain your financial form data.

Providing this information is voluntary. You may access your Member Area without completing the onboarding form, but your personalised plan will not be generated. You can leave any individual question blank.

Membership periods and data retention

We don’t keep your information forever — and we don’t think we should. Data retention periods are different for each plan, and depend on whether you are a free or paid member.

  • 90-Day Plan (free plan) — your account and your My 90-Day Plan are kept for 90 days from the day you created your account. This clock is fixed. Signing in again does not extend it, and there is no way to extend it. On day 90 your account, your answers and your plan are automatically and permanently deleted, and they cannot be restored afterwards. If you want to keep your plan, save or print it while your account is open.
  • The 90-Day Companion (90 days, paid) — your account is active for 90 days from purchase. On day 83 we email you your kit. On day 89 we send a short reminder. On day 90 the season closes and everything personal is permanently deleted. There is no archive period, and after day 90 we cannot restore your account, your plan or your documents — your kit is the copy you keep.
  • Income Builder, Wealth Architect, Business Engine — the same model of a defined active period followed by deletion will apply once these products launch. Specific periods will be published here before each product goes live.

What happens on day 90: On day 90 we delete your plan, your answers, your documents, and everything personal inside them. What stays is the payment and consent receipt our accounting law requires — no plan, no debts, no amounts. Your kit is yours and works without us.

Your kit, and why deletion costs you nothing. On day 83 — seven days before the season closes — we email you a single HTML file containing your plan, your progress, your debt-free date and your letters. It is self-contained: it opens in any browser without an internet connection, it does not call back to our servers, and it keeps working even if Debt-Free.World ceases to exist. Deletion removes our copy, not yours. Please save it somewhere you will find it again, because it cannot be regenerated after day 90.

The receipt we keep, and what is in it. After the deletion, exactly one record about you remains in the system that runs your membership. It has six fields and nothing else: your email address, the Stripe session identifier for the purchase, whether terms consent was required, whether you accepted it, when you accepted it, and the date the deletion ran. Finnish accounting law requires proof of payment and consent to be retained for seven years, which is how long this record is kept and the only reason it exists.

It contains no plan, no debts, no amounts, no name, no onboarding answers and no documents. No page you can log into reads it, and it is not used to contact you.

This retention schedule applies to your email address, your onboarding answers, your generated plan, and any letters or documents you created with us.

Other data retention periods:

  • Newsletter email address — retained in Brevo until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Payment and consent receipt — the six fields described above, retained as required by Finnish accounting law (7 years)

Research and impact measurement

Part of the mission of Debt-Free.World is to demonstrate that accessible financial guidance creates measurable, real-world change. To support this mission, shortly before your account data reaches its scheduled deletion date, we extract a small set of non-identifying statistics from it — for example, your country, the type of debt you had, which tools you used, and whether you reached your goal. We use this anonymised data for:

  • Statistical research and analysis of debt patterns, recovery trajectories, and outcomes
  • Impact measurement and reporting
  • Service improvement and product development
  • Grant applications, funding proposals, and social impact reporting
  • Academic or non-profit collaboration on financial wellbeing research

How we protect you in research use: This anonymised data is completely separated from your name, email address, and any free-text answers, and stored independently of your personal account record. It is not pseudonymised — meaning we keep no key, table, or reference anywhere that could link it back to you. It cannot reasonably be used to identify you as an individual. This process never delays or prevents the deletion of your personal data on its scheduled date. We will not share identifiable personal data with researchers, partners, or funders — ever.

What the anonymous record actually contains. It is a single row of fourteen fields and it has the same shape for everyone: the version of the record format; the month your season started, as a month only and never a day; your country as a two-letter code; your language; whether you joined as a paying member or through Community Access; which companion mode you used; your household type; how many children you have, recorded as 0, 1, 2 or 3+; your employment situation; a start snapshot and an end snapshot, each holding only ranges (total debt, number of debts, monthly surplus, projected months to debt-free, and whether enforcement was involved); how much your projected payoff time improved, as a range; four engagement counts, each as a range; and the fact that the season completed.

Household type, number of children and employment situation come from answers you already gave during onboarding. We did not add any new question to collect them. Nothing in the row is an exact figure, an exact date or a free-text answer.

Small groups are never shown. When these rows are counted, any group of fewer than 20 rows is not shown in any form. The number is not rounded, not banded and not published elsewhere — our internal tool returns a suppression marker in place of the count. A breakdown may combine at most two characteristics at a time. Both limits are enforced in our code, not left to policy or to the judgment of whoever runs the query.

What we never collect. We do not ask for and do not store your age, your date of birth, your gender, or any location more precise than your country — neither in your account nor in the anonymous record. Free-text answers you write during onboarding are stored in your account only to generate your plan, are deleted with it, and never enter the anonymous record.
